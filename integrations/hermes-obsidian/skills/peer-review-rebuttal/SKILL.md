# Peer Review & Rebuttal Specialist

## Mission
Convert difficult peer review decisions into accepted papers through structured, respectful, evidence-backed rebuttals and meticulous manuscript revision tracking.

## Core Rules for Rebuttal

### 1. The Three Golden Principles
1. **Answer completely:** Every single critique, question, or minor suggestion must receive an individual, explicit response. Never group together or ignore difficult comments.
2. **Polite and respectful tone:** Treat reviewers as expert partners helping to improve the manuscript. Never argue emotionally or disparage a reviewer's comprehension.
3. **Show, don't just tell:** Never say merely "We have fixed this in the text." Always quote the exact revised text excerpt alongside section numbers, page numbers, and line numbers.

### 2. Standard 3-Part Response Architecture
For every numbered comment, structure the response into three distinct visual components:
1. **Reviewer Comment:** Quoted in full in bold/italics.
2. **Author Response:** Sincere acknowledgment, scientific reasoning, and summary of additional experiments or adjustments performed.
3. **Manuscript Revision Excerpt:** Exact blockquote of the revised text as it appears in the revised manuscript:
   > *"Manuscript Revision (Section X.X, Page Y, Lines Z–Z): ..."*

### 3. Handling Disagreements Diplomatically
When a reviewer suggests an incorrect interpretation or requests unreasonable out-of-scope work:
- **Validate first:** *"We understand the reviewer's concern regarding... and agree that this is a valuable dimension of research."*
- **Explain with evidence:** Provide empirical evidence, mathematical proofs, or citations showing why the current approach was chosen.
- **Clarify in the manuscript:** Concede that if the reviewer was confused, future readers will be too. Add clarifying text in the Discussion or Limitations section:
  *"To prevent potential misunderstanding for future readers, we have added a clarifying statement in Section 4.4..."*

## Rebuttal Package Checklist
- [ ] Cover letter to Editor summarizing main revisions and thanking reviewers.
- [ ] Numbered point-by-point responses to Editor comments.
- [ ] Numbered point-by-point responses to Reviewer #1.
- [ ] Numbered point-by-point responses to Reviewer #2 (and Reviewer #3).
- [ ] Clean revised manuscript file.
- [ ] Marked / Track-changes copy highlighting revisions in color.
