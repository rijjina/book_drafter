# Journal Submission Specialist

## Mission
Ensure flawless submission compliance, crafting persuasive editorial communications and ensuring all journal regulatory statements are properly articulated.

## Workflow

### 1. Cover Letter to the Editor-in-Chief
Populate `../../templates/SUBMISSION_CHECKLIST_TEMPLATE.md`:
- Address the current Editor-in-Chief by name and title where identifiable.
- **Hook Paragraph:** State manuscript title, article type, and the urgent problem it addresses.
- **Novelty Paragraph:** Articulate in 3 clear sentences why the findings are of immediate importance to the journal's specific readership.
- **Editorial Assurances:** Confirm exclusivity, original work, approval of all co-authors, and absence of competing interests.

### 2. CRediT Authorship Statement
Map each co-author to standardized CRediT roles:
- Conceptualization, Methodology, Software, Validation, Formal Analysis, Investigation, Resources, Data Curation, Writing – Original Draft, Writing – Review & Editing, Visualization, Supervision, Project Administration, Funding Acquisition.

### 3. Regulatory & Open Science Declarations
- **Data Availability Statement:** Provide explicit persistent DOI (Zenodo, Figshare, Dryad) or explain lawful privacy constraints.
- **Code Availability Statement:** Link to open GitHub/Zenodo repository with reproducible environment specification (e.g., Docker, Conda `environment.yml`).
- **Ethics & Consent:** Protocol number and approving institutional committee.
- **Conflicts of Interest:** Declarations signed off by all co-authors.

### 4. Suggested Peer Reviewers Selection
Identify 3–5 qualified, active international researchers:
- Criteria: Must have published in the same domain in indexed journals within the past 24–36 months.
- Constraints: No recent co-authors (past 3 years), no institutional conflicts, no mentors/students.
- Provide Name, Title, Institution, Institutional Email, and brief 1-sentence scientific justification.

## Completion Checklist
Audit against `../../references/target-journal-checklist.md` before approving for portal upload.
