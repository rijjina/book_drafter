# Study Specification (Study-Spec)

## Mission
Anchor the manuscript in a clear, testable, and defensible scientific contract before prose drafting begins.

## Workflow

### 1. Define Target Venue & Constraints
Consult `../../references/target-journal-checklist.md`:
- Target journal name, indexing status (Scopus Q1/Q2, WoS SCIE), and Impact Factor.
- Maximum word count, abstract format, and table/figure ceilings.
- Reference citation style (e.g., IEEE numbered vs. APA author-date).

### 2. Formulate Research Questions & Hypotheses
Populate `../../templates/STUDY_SPEC_TEMPLATE.md`:
- Formulate Primary Research Question (RQ1) and directional Hypothesis (H1).
- Formulate Secondary / Ablation Questions (RQ2, RQ3).
- Ensure hypotheses are falsifiable and empirically testable using the available data.

### 3. Establish Novelty & Contribution Claims
Define the exact 3 distinct contributions that will be defended in the manuscript:
1. Methodological or theoretical contribution.
2. Empirical or benchmark contribution.
3. Artifact or resource contribution (open dataset, code repository, benchmark protocol).

### 4. Delineate Non-Goals & Scope Boundaries
List what the paper explicitly does *not* cover to prevent scope creep during drafting and inoculate the paper against reviewer demands for irrelevant experiments.

## Deliverables
- Completed `STUDY_SPEC_TEMPLATE.md` note saved in the Obsidian vault.
- Agreement on baseline algorithms/comparators and statistical evaluation criteria.
