# Advisory Criterion Catalog

Use this catalog to organize an assessment, not as proof of a current official
rule. Replace or qualify each route-sensitive criterion with the selected
institution's verified rule when `CURRENT_RULES` is active.

## Shared Academic And Integrity Dimensions

- correct, current, complete, and appropriately bounded content;
- coherent central concept, structure, progression, and independent usability;
- analysis, comparison, synthesis, limitations, and defensible conclusions;
- visible author contribution, relevant experience, or research without
  invented attribution;
- traceable claims, citations, bibliography, figures, tables, permissions, and
  reuse status;
- consistent terminology, formal readable Thai, accessibility, and navigation;
- ethical authorship, no plagiarism or disguised translation, no duplicate
  submission, and no fabricated evidence.

## Book

Assess disciplinary scope, intended readers, central scholarly problem,
coverage and depth, coherent author viewpoint, contribution to the field,
currency, national/international usability, publication form, peer review,
dissemination, bibliographic identity, and rights. Do not demand course mapping
unless the selected official rule explicitly does so.

## Textbook

Assess the defined course or course segment, author teaching scope, content
coverage, sequence, learning support, examples/activities where appropriate,
independent readability, scholarly depth, synthesis, author contribution,
currency, publication/dissemination, and rights. Require CLO/PLO/OBE/TQF data
only when the governing institution requires it.

## Teaching Handout

Assess course identity, assigned teaching scope, systematic coverage,
instructional sequence, explanation and supporting media, source breadth,
language, author analysis, usefulness, use evidence, and the selected
institution's definition of เอกสารประกอบการสอน. Do not automatically apply a
rubric from another university.

## Teaching Notes

Assess the same course-linked foundations plus completeness for learner
self-study, depth beyond a basic handout, research/source breadth, author ideas,
teaching value, use evidence, and the selected institution's definition of
เอกสารคำสอน. Treat any internal A-equivalent target as advisory unless the
governing rule defines it.

## Outline, Chapter, And Whole-Work Anchors

- Outline: title/scope alignment, sequence, coverage, evidence plan, rights
  plan, contribution, outcomes, examples, and front/back matter.
- Chapter: purpose, correctness, evidence, explanation, depth, synthesis,
  contribution, examples, citations, rights, summary, and transitions.
- Whole work: unity, chapter balance, duplication/gaps, terminology,
  cross-references, bibliography, index/navigation, contribution, dissemination,
  peer review, publication evidence, and route artifacts.
