## Preservation Contract

- Preserve the author's established level of formality, paragraph rhythm, terminology, heading hierarchy, citation presentation, and preferred transitions when academically sound.
- Preserve distinctive synthesis, examples, tables, and argument order unless an approved QC item requires a change.
- Revise style only to correct accuracy, integrity, logic, scope, evidence, citation, terminology, clarity/accessibility, approved QC issues, or mandatory institutional, publisher, or official rules.
- Do not imitate extraction noise, OCR errors, accidental repetition, inconsistent formatting, unsupported claims, or noncompliant citation forms.
- When the measured baseline is sparse or mixed, treat this profile as evidence rather than a command and record the editorial decision.
